EDUCATION / 01

AI Tutor Platform

Personal learning support for a global digital education provider.

Challenge: Learners needed tailored guidance between live sessions, while educators needed a scalable way to support more cohorts.

Solution

A curriculum-aware tutor with Socratic guidance, practice feedback, and educator escalation.

Architecture

RAG pipeline, learning graph, LLM evaluator, teacher dashboard, and event analytics.

Results

42% improvement in course completion and a 29% lift in active learning time.

ROI

3.7x return in the first year from retention and lower support load.
