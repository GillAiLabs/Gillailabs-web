Effective date: July 16, 2026

This policy explains how Gill AI Labs may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit gillailabs.com.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They can help websites remember preferences, understand performance, and provide relevant functionality.

Cookies we may use

  • Strictly necessary cookies to keep the website functioning.
  • Preference cookies, such as the selected light or dark display theme.
  • Analytics cookies, if enabled, to understand aggregate website usage.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality. Where consent is required for non-essential cookies, we will request it before placing them.
