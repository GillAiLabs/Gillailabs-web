The lab mindset

Explore widely. Introduce carefully.

New model capabilities arrive quickly. Our job is to separate genuinely useful advances from impressive but fragile demonstrations.

Prototype to learn

Small experiments make the practical limits of a model, integration, or interaction visible quickly.

Evaluate to decide

We test against real tasks, failure modes, and operating constraints rather than an idealized prompt.

Productize with intent

Only patterns that improve a meaningful workflow earn their way into a delivery roadmap.
