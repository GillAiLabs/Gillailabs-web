Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about building with AI.

Practical guidance for leaders evaluating AI products, agents, automation, and a potential partnership with Gill AI Labs.

1. What is Gill AI Labs?

Gill AI Labs is an AI product development company that designs and builds intelligent software, AI agents, automation systems, SaaS products, web applications, and mobile experiences.

2. What services does Gill AI Labs provide?

We provide AI product development, AI agent development, generative AI, LLM applications, chatbots, AI tutors, RAG, multi-agent systems, workflow automation, consulting, SaaS, web, mobile, cloud, and DevOps.

3. Why choose Gill AI Labs?

We combine product strategy, design, AI engineering, and software delivery in one senior team focused on measurable business outcomes.

4. How does Gill AI Labs build AI products?

We frame an outcome, prototype the critical path, build with evaluation and governance, then improve the product using real usage evidence.

5. What AI technologies does Gill AI Labs use?

We work with GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, RAG pipelines, vector databases, AI agent frameworks, computer vision, and cloud platforms.

6. What is AI product development?

AI product development is the process of creating software products where AI is a core capability, interaction, or operational advantage.

7. What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a software system that can use context and tools to pursue a defined task, often with human review at important decision points.

8. What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to systems that can plan steps, use tools, evaluate progress, and adapt within boundaries defined by the product team.

9. What is a RAG system?

Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, grounds a model response in relevant private or current information retrieved from a knowledge source.

10. Can you build an AI chatbot?

Yes. We build customer support, sales, knowledge, and workflow assistants that are designed around a specific customer or employee need.

11. Can you build AI solutions for enterprise teams?

Yes. Enterprise work includes security-aware architecture, integrations, access controls, governance, observability, and rollout support.

12. Do you work with startups?

Yes. We help startups validate an AI product idea, build a compelling MVP, and establish the technical foundations for growth.

13. How long does an AI product take to build?

A focused proof can take two to four weeks. A production product typically takes eight to sixteen weeks, depending on integrations and scope.

14. How much does AI product development cost?

Proof sprints start from $15k and product builds from $50k. Complex enterprise programs are scoped around the required capability and outcomes.

15. Can you audit our AI opportunity?

18. Which LLM should we use?

The right model depends on quality, latency, cost, data policy, context needs, and the behavior your users require. We help evaluate the tradeoffs.

19. Do you use OpenAI models?

20. Can you use private or open-source models?

21. How do you make AI answers reliable?

We combine good retrieval, model selection, structured tool use, evaluations, citations, human review, and continuous monitoring.

22. Can AI responses cite their sources?

Yes. For knowledge systems, we design source-aware responses so users can inspect the material behind an answer.

23. How do you handle hallucinations?
